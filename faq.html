FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about staking and delegating with PortZero.

Is delegating safe?

Delegation is non-custodial: your tokens remain in your wallet and PortZero never takes custody of them.

How do I earn rewards?

Rewards accrue to delegators based on the validator's performance and its commission rate.

Can I undelegate at any time?

Yes, though the network's unbonding period applies before tokens become available again.

What commission does PortZero charge?

See the validator details on the Delegate page for the current commission rate.

How do I get support?

Reach out through our community channels below and we will help you.

Does PortZero take part in governance?

Yes, PortZero aims to vote on network proposals and share its reasoning with delegators.
